# 🧾 Reseller API: Customers, orders and billing

All examples use `$GH_KEY` for your API key and `$CT` for a customer token. Base URL: `https://greathost.es/api/reseller/v1`.

## Products

### `GET /products`
The products you have on sale that the API can sell. No customer token needed.

```json
{
  "success": true,
  "products": [
    {
      "id": "8c1f…",
      "type": "vps",
      "game": "vps-v2",
      "plan": "kvm-1",
      "region": "es",
      "cycleDays": 30,
      "name": "KVM 1",
      "description": "",
      "priceCents": 650,
      "priceLabel": "6.50",
      "currency": "EUR",
      "billingCycle": "monthly",
      "resources": { "cores": 2, "memory": 8192, "disk": 40960, "trafficTb": 5 },
      "inStock": true
    }
  ]
}
```

- `id` is the **productId** you use to order.
- `type` is `gameserver` or `vps`.
- `resources.memory` and `resources.disk` are in MB.

For VPS, `GET /vps/catalog` gives you everything needed for an order form (operating systems, stock, features). See **API - VPS**.

## Customers

### `POST /customers`: register a customer
```json
{
  "email": "jane@example.com",
  "password": "a-strong-password",
  "username": "jane",
  "fullName": "Jane Doe",
  "country": "ES"
}
```

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `email` | yes | Unique in your store. |
| `password` | yes | At least 8 characters. |
| `username` | no | 3-40 characters: letters, numbers, `_ . -`. |
| `fullName` | no | |
| `country` | no | 2-letter code. |

Answer (`201`): the customer and their `customerToken`. Customers created through the API are considered verified: they do not need to confirm their email.

Errors: `EMAIL_TAKEN` (409), `INVALID_USERNAME`, `INVALID_REQUEST`.

The same customer can also sign in to your storefront with that email and password.

### `POST /customers/login`: sign in a customer
```json
{ "email": "jane@example.com", "password": "a-strong-password" }
```
Answer: the customer and a fresh `customerToken`. Error: `INVALID_CREDENTIALS` (401). Limited to 10 attempts per email every 10 minutes.

### `GET /customers/me`
Needs `X-Customer-Token`. Returns the customer.

## Orders

### `POST /orders`: buy a service for the customer
Needs `X-Customer-Token` and `Idempotency-Key`.

```json
{ "productId": "8c1f…", "serviceName": "Survival server" }
```

What happens:
1. The price is taken from your price list (never from the request).
2. The wholesale price is charged to your credit.
3. The service is created.

Answer (`201`):
```json
{ "success": true, "service": { "id": "a41e…", "type": "gameserver", "status": "active", "...": "..." } }
```

For a **VPS** the body has extra fields. See **API - VPS**.

Errors:
| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REQUIRED` | Missing or invalid `Idempotency-Key` header. |
| `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT` | You reused a key with a different request. |
| `IDEMPOTENCY_IN_PROGRESS` | The first request with this key is still running. Wait and retry. |
| `PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND` | The product does not exist, is hidden, or cannot be sold through the API. |
| `ORDER_REFUSED` | The order was refused (store limit reached, out of stock, not enough credit…). See `message`. |
| `PROVISION_FAILED` (502) | The order was recorded but the service could not be created. It is **retried automatically**; follow its `status`. |

## Services

### `GET /services`
All the customer's services. Each has an `id`, `type`, `status`, `priceCents`, `nextDueAt` and more. VPS also include a `billing` block.

### `GET /services/:id`
One service, with its invoices.

**Service status**: `pending`, `provisioning`, `active`, `overdue`, `suspended`, `terminated`, `failed`, `cancelled`. Their meaning is explained in **Customers, services and invoices**.

### `POST /services/:id/renew`: renew a service
Pays the next renewal from your credit. Charge your customer first in your own app, then call this.

Answer: the updated service (new `nextDueAt`) and the paid invoice.

- **Game servers**: the renewal invoice appears 3 days before the renewal date. Before that you get `NOTHING_TO_RENEW`.
- **VPS**: you can renew at any moment, even after the due date while the VPS still exists, but never more than one cycle in advance (`ALREADY_RENEWED`).

Errors: `INSUFFICIENT_CREDIT` (402), `NOTHING_TO_RENEW`, `NOT_RENEWABLE`, `ALREADY_RENEWED`, `SERVICE_GONE` (410).

### `POST /services/:id/cancel`: cancel a service
```json
{ "reason": "Customer request" }
```
By default the service keeps running until the end of the period already paid, and is then deleted. Answer: the service and `endsAt`.

To delete it **right now, with all its data** (cannot be undone):
```json
{ "immediate": true, "confirm": true }
```

### `POST /services/:id/resume`
Withdraws a scheduled cancellation, as long as the service has not ended yet.

## What happens if you do not renew
- **Game servers**: the service becomes `overdue` on the renewal date, `suspended` after the grace period, and is deleted a few days later. Renew at any point before deletion to bring it back.
- **VPS**: see **API - VPS → Billing**.

## Invoices
Every order, renewal and paid add-on creates an invoice in your store, visible in the **Invoices** tab and in each service (`GET /services/:id`). They are your record of what you sold and what it cost you. Your customers are billed by your own app, so these invoices cannot be paid by card from the storefront.
