# 📈 Earnings and credit

## Overview
The **Earnings** tab shows how your store is doing, with every amount split into what your customers paid, what GreatHost charged you and what you keep.

## Lifetime figures
| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lifetime revenue** | Everything your customers have paid you. |
| **Lifetime wholesale cost** | Everything GreatHost has charged you for those services. |
| **Lifetime profit** | The difference. |
| **GreatHost credit** | Your current balance, in euros and coins. |

## Recurring figures
- **Monthly revenue**: what your active services bring in each month.
- **Monthly wholesale cost**: what they cost you each month.
- **Monthly profit**: the difference.
- **Active services** and **This month billed**.

The box below tells you how many months of renewals your credit covers. **Top up before it runs out**, otherwise your customers' renewals fail. 100 coins = 1 €.

## Ledger
The **Ledger** lists every movement, newest first:

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **sale_gross** | A payment from a customer. |
| **wholesale_cost** | What GreatHost charged you for it. |
| **platform_fee** | Commission, only in platform billing mode. |
| **margin_credit** | Profit added to your balance, only in platform billing mode. |
| **payout** | Money paid out to you. |
| **adjustment** | A manual correction by GreatHost. |

## Payouts
In the default billing mode your customers pay into **your own** Stripe or PayPal account, so your profit is already yours and **payouts do not apply**.

If your store uses **platform billing**, your profit builds up as a balance and you can request a payout from this tab:

1. Enter the **Amount** (at least the minimum shown).
2. Choose the **Method**: GreatHost credit (instant), PayPal or bank transfer.
3. For PayPal or bank transfer, enter your PayPal email or IBAN as **Destination**.
4. Click **Request payout**. Its status is shown in the **History** table.
