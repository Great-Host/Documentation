# 👥 Customers, services and invoices

## Customers
The **Customers** tab lists everyone registered in your store (from the storefront or through the API). Search by email or username, and click **Open** to see a customer's details, services and lifetime spend.

From the customer window you can:

- **Send password reset**: emails them a link to choose a new password.
- **Suspend account**: they can no longer sign in or order. Their services are not touched.
- **Reactivate account**: undo a suspension.

## Services
The **Services** tab lists every service you have sold, with what the customer pays, what you pay, your profit and the next renewal date. A **not renewing** badge means the customer has asked to cancel.

Click **Manage** to see the details and the live state of the service, and to:

- **Suspend**: stop the service (for example while you investigate abuse). The data is kept.
- **Reactivate**: start a suspended service again.
- **Terminate & delete**: delete the service and **all its data** immediately. This cannot be undone.

> **Note:** Every service is also visible in your normal GreatHost dashboard. Do not delete or cancel it from there: always use the Reseller Panel (or the API) so your store stays in sync.

### Service status
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **pending** | Ordered, waiting for payment. |
| **provisioning** | Paid, being created. |
| **active** | Running normally. |
| **overdue** | The renewal date passed and the invoice is unpaid. Still running for now. |
| **suspended** | Stopped for non-payment or by you. Data kept. |
| **terminated** | Deleted. |
| **failed** | Paid, but it could not be created. See below. |
| **cancelled** | Cancelled before it was ever created. |

## Invoices
The **Invoices** tab lists every invoice of your store: first payments (**new**), **renewal** invoices and **upgrade** invoices (plan changes and add-ons bought through the API).

- **Mark paid**: record a payment received outside Stripe or PayPal. The service is created or renewed immediately and your credit is charged.
- **Void**: cancel an invoice that should not be paid.

## Renewals and non-payment
For monthly game servers:

1. **3 days before** the renewal date, a renewal invoice is issued and emailed to the customer.
2. **On the renewal date**, if it is still unpaid, the service becomes **overdue** but keeps running.
3. **After the grace period** (3 days by default) the service is **suspended**. Data is kept.
4. **After a few more days** (7 by default) the service is **terminated** and its data deleted.

As soon as the customer pays, the renewal date moves forward and a suspended service is reactivated automatically.

> **Note:** The renewal of the server itself is charged to your credit on its own renewal date, like any other GreatHost service. If a customer stops paying you, suspend or terminate the service so you do not keep paying for it. The steps above do it for you automatically.

### VPS renewals
VPS work a little differently, because a VPS cannot keep running unpaid:

1. **3 days before** the renewal date, a renewal invoice is issued and emailed to the customer. They can also renew earlier from **Plan & billing → Renew now**.
2. When the customer pays, the VPS is renewed and **your credit is charged** at that moment.
3. **On the renewal date**, if it was not paid, the VPS is **stopped** and its IP is released. Data is kept.
4. The customer can still pay for a few more days; the VPS then comes back automatically, possibly with a new IP.
5. After those days the VPS and its data are **deleted**.

> **Important:** Before a customer pays a VPS renewal, plan change or extra IP, we check that your credit can cover it. If it cannot, the payment is refused, the customer sees "This payment cannot be taken right now" and **you receive an email**. If your credit runs out in the few seconds between the payment and the charge, the payment is kept, the invoice shows as paid and the change is **applied automatically as soon as you top up**. Top up quickly in that case: until then the customer does not get what they paid for.

More details in **Selling VPS**. VPS sold through the API follow the same idea, explained in **Reseller API → VPS**.

## Cancellations
- A customer can **schedule** a cancellation from your client area. The service keeps running until the end of the period they paid for, then it is deleted and not renewed. They can withdraw the cancellation before that date.
- Only you can delete a service immediately (**Terminate & delete**).

## When a service fails to be created
If a customer paid but their service could not be created, it is marked **failed**. The most common reason is that **your credit was too low**.

- Top up your credit: failed services are **retried automatically** for a while.
- If it still fails, open a support ticket with GreatHost.
