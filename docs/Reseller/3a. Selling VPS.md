# 🖥️ Selling VPS

## Overview
You can sell our **VPS KVM** servers under your own brand. Your customers buy them in your storefront, pay you, and manage them from a complete VPS control panel in your client area. You can also sell them from your own app with the Reseller API (see **Reseller API → VPS**).

A VPS is a full virtual server with root access, its own IPv4 address, NVMe storage and a monthly traffic allowance, in the location you choose to sell (for example Spain or the United States).

## Before you start
1. Your store must be in the default billing mode (your customers pay into **your** Stripe or PayPal). VPS are not shown in stores using platform billing.
2. Keep enough GreatHost **credit**: every VPS is charged to it when it is created and each time it renews.
3. Decide which VPS sections your customers get and the price of extra IPs (see below).

## Step 1: Put VPS plans on sale
1. Open **Products & pricing**.
2. Find the **VPS KVM** groups, one per location (for example **VPS KVM Spain**).
3. Set **Your price** for the plans you want to sell and click **Save**.

The price is per **renewal period**, normally **30 days** (not a calendar month). Your store shows it as "per 30 days".

> **Tip:** Selling several plans of the same location lets your customers move up to a bigger plan later without changing server.

## Step 2: Choose what your customers can do
Open **Storefront → Store behaviour**. VPS use the same switches as game servers, plus three of their own:

| Switch | What it allows on a VPS |
|---|---|
| Power controls | Start, restart, shut down, force off, and scheduled tasks. |
| Live console | The browser console. |
| Backups | Manual and automatic backups. |
| Reinstall | Reinstalling the system (off by default: it erases the disk). |
| Rename | Changing the hostname. |
| **Snapshots** | Quick restore points. |
| **Advanced** | Rescue mode, ISO images, firmware and boot order. |
| **Plan changes and additional IPs** | Moving to another plan and buying extra IPv4 addresses. |

**Price of an additional IPv4**: what your customers pay per extra IP and per renewal period. Leave it empty to charge your cost (no profit). It is never charged below your cost.

Click **Save settings**.

## How your customer buys a VPS
1. They choose a VPS plan in your store and click **Order now**.
2. They pick the **operating system** (Debian, Ubuntu, AlmaLinux, Rocky Linux…) and, if they want, a **hostname** and their **SSH public key**.
3. They pay the first period by card or PayPal.
4. The VPS is created automatically in a few minutes. Their panel shows the progress.
5. The **root password** is generated for them and shown in the panel (**Overview → Show password**).

Your credit is charged the moment the VPS is created. If the creation fails, your credit is refunded and the creation is retried automatically.

## The customer's VPS panel
| Tab | What it includes |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | IP, username, password, SSH command, browser console, live CPU/memory/disk, 24-hour usage graphs, traffic of the month and recent activity. |
| **Network** | Network settings and additional IPv4 addresses. |
| **Backups** | Create, restore and delete backups; automatic backups daily, weekly or monthly. |
| **Snapshots** | Take a restore point and go back to it. |
| **Settings** | Hostname, rescue mode, ISO images, firmware, boot order, scheduled tasks, reinstall and cancellation. |
| **Plan & billing** | Renew, change plan, invoices, cancel or undo a cancellation. |

Power buttons (**Start**, **Restart**, **Shut down**, **Force off**) are always at the top. Tabs and buttons you switched off do not appear.

> **Note:** On your own domain, the browser console may ask your customer to sign in. The panel shows a temporary user name and password for it: they only open the console of that VPS and expire automatically. The console page itself opens on our infrastructure address.

## Billing
### Renewals
- **3 days before** the end of the period, a renewal invoice is created and emailed to your customer. They can also renew earlier from **Plan & billing → Renew now** (at most one period in advance).
- When your customer pays, the VPS gets another period and **your credit is charged at that moment**.
- VPS in your storefront **never renew on their own**: you never pay GreatHost for a period your customer did not pay you.

### If the customer does not pay
1. **On the renewal date** the VPS is **stopped** and its IP address is released. The service shows as **suspended**. Data is kept.
2. For a few more days the customer can still pay; the VPS starts again automatically, **possibly with a different IP**.
3. After that the VPS and all its data are **deleted** and the service shows as **terminated**.

### Plan changes and extra IPs
- When a customer changes plan or adds an IP, an invoice is created for **the rest of the current period**. The change is applied **as soon as it is paid**.
- From the next renewal the new price is included in the renewal invoice (plan + extra IPs).
- Moving to a cheaper plan or one with a smaller disk may not be possible; the panel only offers the plans the VPS can move to.
- A change that costs nothing for the rest of the period is applied immediately.

### Your credit protects you, and your customers
Before your customer pays a renewal, a plan change or an extra IP, we check that your credit can cover what it costs you:

- **If it cannot**, the payment is not taken. Your customer sees "This payment cannot be taken right now" and **you receive an email** to top up.
- **In the rare case** your credit runs out between the payment and the charge, your customer's payment is kept, the invoice shows as paid, and the renewal or change is **applied automatically as soon as you top up**. Top up quickly: until then your customer has paid for something they do not have yet.

## Managing a customer's VPS
In the **Services** tab, click **Manage** on a VPS to see its state and IP, and to:

- **Suspend**: stop it (for example while you look into abuse). It is not renewed or reactivated automatically while you keep it suspended.
- **Reactivate**: start it again.
- **Terminate & delete**: delete it now, with all its data.

Every VPS also appears in your normal GreatHost dashboard. **Do not renew, cancel or delete it from there**: always use the Reseller Panel, so your store and your customer stay in sync.

## Cancellations
Your customer can cancel from **Plan & billing**. The VPS keeps working until the end of the period they paid, then it is deleted. They can undo the cancellation until then.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can my customer choose their root password?**
Not when ordering: a secure one is generated and shown in the panel. They can set their own when they reinstall.

**Why did the IP address change?**
The VPS was not renewed on time, so its IP was released. When it was renewed it received a new one.

**Can I sell Windows VPS?**
No. Only Linux systems that install automatically are available.

**My customer paid but the VPS was not renewed.**
Check your credit in **Overview → Wholesale credit** and top up. Pending renewals are completed automatically on the next billing run (every 30 minutes).

**Where do I see what each VPS costs me?**
In **Services** (*You pay* column) and in **Earnings → Ledger**.
