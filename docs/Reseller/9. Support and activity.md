# 🎧 Support and activity

## Support tickets
You are the **first line of support** for your own customers. When **Let customers open support tickets** is enabled, the tickets your customers open from your client area appear in the **Support** tab.

1. Click **Open** on a ticket to read the conversation.
2. Write your answer and click **Send reply**. The customer sees it in the support section of your client area.
3. Click **Close ticket** when it is solved.

If the problem is on our side (a server that does not start, a node issue, a billing error), open a ticket with GreatHost from your normal dashboard: [https://greathost.es/support](https://greathost.es/support).

## Activity log
The **Activity** tab records everything that happens in your store: orders, payments, provisioning, suspensions, price changes, API calls that change something, and more. Each entry shows **who** did it:

| Who | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **customer** | One of your customers. |
| **owner** | You, from the panel or with your API key. |
| **admin** | GreatHost staff. |
| **system** | An automatic process (renewals, suspensions, retries). |

Use it to answer questions like "who deleted that file?" or "when did this service renew?".

## Good practices
- Keep your **credit** topped up.
- Answer tickets quickly: your customers do not know GreatHost exists.
- Keep **Reinstall** disabled unless your customers need it.
- Publish clear **terms** and a **privacy policy** for your store.
