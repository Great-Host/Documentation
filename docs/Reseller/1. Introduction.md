# 🏪 Reseller Programme

## Overview
The **Reseller Programme** lets you run your own hosting brand on top of GreatHost. You create a store with your own name, colours and domain, you choose your own prices, and you sell game servers and VPS to your own customers.

Your customers never see GreatHost: they register in **your** store, pay **you**, and manage their services from **your** client area.

You can sell in two ways, and you can use both at the same time:

| Channel | What it is | What you can sell |
|---|---|---|
| **Your storefront** | A ready-made, white-label website and client area, hosted for you. | Game servers and **VPS (KVM)** |
| **The Reseller API** | An API to build your own website, app or bot on top of GreatHost. | Game servers and **VPS (KVM)** |

## How the money works
A worked example, using round numbers:

1. A Minecraft plan costs you **1.00 €** (the GreatHost *wholesale price*).
2. You list it in your store at **1.50 €**.
3. Your customer pays **1.50 €** straight into **your own** Stripe or PayPal account.
4. GreatHost charges **1.00 €** from your GreatHost **credit** and creates the server.
5. The remaining **0.50 €** is your profit, and it is already in your account.

> **Note:** GreatHost never touches your customers' money. We only charge the wholesale price to your GreatHost credit.

### Your credit pays for your customers' services
Every service you sell is paid to GreatHost from your credit (coins), both when it is created and every time it renews. **Keep enough credit topped up**: if your credit runs out, new orders cannot be created and renewals fail.

- The **Overview** tab shows your balance and your *runway* (how many months your credit covers at your current usage).
- We email you when your credit is too low to create or renew a service.
- You can top up at any time from **Buy coins**.

## Where the services live
Every service you sell is a real GreatHost service **in your own GreatHost account**. That means:

- It appears in your normal GreatHost dashboard, so you can inspect and support it with the tools you already know.
- Your customer manages it through your store (or your own app), never through GreatHost.

## What you need to start
- A GreatHost account with a **verified email address**.
- Some GreatHost **credit** to pay for the first services.
- A **Stripe** and/or **PayPal** account to receive payments in your storefront.
- *(Optional)* your **own domain** for the store.
- *(Optional)* a server of your own if you want to use the **Reseller API**.
