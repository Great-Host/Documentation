# 💶 Products and pricing

## Overview
The **Products & pricing** tab lists every product GreatHost lets you resell, grouped by product, with each of its plans. For every plan you see:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Your cost** | What GreatHost charges your credit for one billing cycle (the wholesale price). |
| **Your price** | What your customer pays you. |
| **Your margin** | Price minus cost: your profit per cycle, in money and in %. |
| **Status** | *on sale*, *hidden* or *not listed*. |

## Setting a price
1. Type your price in the **Your price** field (for example `2.50`). If you leave it empty, the suggested price shown in grey is used.
2. Click **Save**. The plan is now on sale.
3. Use **Hide** / **Show** to take a plan off your store without losing its price.

### Price limits
- You can **never sell below cost**. A price under the wholesale price is refused.
- Prices must stay between a minimum and a maximum markup over cost. The current band is shown at the top of the tab (for example *cost + 0 %* to *cost + 900 %*).

### Changing every price at once
Use **Set every price at cost + X % → Apply to all** to re-price your whole catalogue with a single markup. You are asked for confirmation first.

## What happens when GreatHost changes a wholesale price
- Your existing customers keep paying the price they bought at.
- If a wholesale price rises above your price, that product is **hidden automatically** so you never sell at a loss. Raise your price and show it again.
- A product that GreatHost stops offering is also hidden. Products that customers still use cannot be deleted: hide them instead.

## Billing cycles
| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *(none)* | Monthly: renews every month. |
| **one-off** | Paid once, never renews. |
| **API only** | Sold only through the Reseller API, see below. |

## VPS (KVM)
You can resell our **VPS KVM** plans, in every location we offer (for example Spain and the United States). They appear in this tab as **VPS KVM Spain**, **VPS KVM United States**, etc., with the **API only** badge.

Things to know about VPS:

- **They are sold only through the Reseller API**, from your own website or app. They are not shown in your storefront.
- The billing cycle is a fixed number of days (normally **30 days**), not a calendar month.
- Lifetime VPS plans and Windows images are not available for resale.
- Price them here like any other product; then sell them with the API. See **Reseller API → VPS**.
