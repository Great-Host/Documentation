# 🌐 Custom domain

## Overview
By default your store is reachable at its preview address (`https://greathost.es/p/<identifier>`). Connect your own domain so your customers never see a GreatHost address.

## Connecting your domain
1. In your DNS provider, create a record for the domain you want to use:
   - **Subdomain** (for example `panel.yourdomain.com`): a `CNAME` record pointing to `control.greathost.es`.
   - **Root domain** (for example `yourdomain.com`): an `A` record pointing to the IP address of `control.greathost.es`.
2. In the Reseller Panel, open the **Domain** tab.
3. Type your domain and click **Connect my domain**.

The SSL certificate (HTTPS) is issued and renewed **automatically**. You do not need to do anything else.

> **Note:** DNS changes can take from a few minutes to a few hours to propagate. If your domain is not ready yet, we keep retrying automatically until it is.

## Domain status
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **active** | Working, with a valid certificate. |
| **pending** | Waiting for your DNS record to propagate. |
| **degraded** / error | Something is wrong. The last error is shown below the status. |

## Troubleshooting
- **It stays pending**: check that your DNS record points exactly where described above. Some providers need up to 24 hours.
- **The certificate failed**: fix the DNS record, then click **Redeploy**.
- **Cloudflare or another proxy**: set the record to *DNS only* while the certificate is being issued.

## Removing the domain
Click **Remove domain**. Your store keeps working at its preview address.
