# 💳 Payments

## Overview
Your customers pay **into your own accounts**. Connect at least one payment provider in the **Payments** tab: without one, your storefront cannot take orders.

GreatHost never sees these funds. We only charge the wholesale price to your GreatHost credit.

## Stripe (cards)
1. In your Stripe dashboard, go to **Developers → API keys**.
2. Copy the **Publishable key** (`pk_live_…`) and the **Secret key** (`sk_live_…`).
3. In the **Payments** tab, tick **Accept Stripe payments** and paste both keys.
4. *(Recommended)* Create a webhook in Stripe pointing to the address shown under **Webhook signing secret**, and paste its signing secret (`whsec_…`). With it, a payment is processed even if the customer closes the page right after paying.
5. Click **Save Stripe**.

## PayPal
1. In the PayPal developer dashboard, create a **REST app**.
2. Copy its **Client ID** and **Client secret**.
3. In the **Payments** tab, tick **Accept PayPal payments**, choose **Live** and paste them.
4. Click **Save PayPal**.

> **Tip:** Use **Sandbox (testing)** mode with PayPal sandbox credentials to test your store without real money. Switch to **Live** before opening.

## Security
- Your secret keys are stored **encrypted** and are never shown again, not even to you. When a key is saved, the field shows a short hint and *Leave empty to keep it*.
- To replace a key, type the new one. To remove a provider completely, click **Disconnect**.

## Payments received outside Stripe or PayPal
If a customer pays you by bank transfer, cash or any other way, open the **Invoices** tab and click **Mark paid** on their invoice. The service is created or renewed exactly as if they had paid by card, and the wholesale price is charged to your credit.

## Billing mode
Most stores use the default mode, where customers pay into your accounts as described above.

In some cases GreatHost can set your store to **platform billing**: your customers then pay GreatHost directly, GreatHost keeps the wholesale price and a small commission on your margin, and your profit is added to your balance so you can request a **payout**. If your store uses this mode, the **Payments** tab tells you so and you do not need to connect your own gateway. Contact support if you are interested.
