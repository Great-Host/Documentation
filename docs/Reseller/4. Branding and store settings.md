# 🎨 Branding and store settings

## Branding
In the **Storefront** tab, under **Branding**, you choose how your store looks:

- **Store name** and **Tagline**
- **Logo URL** and **Favicon URL**: `https://` images. Without a logo, your initials are used.
- **Colours**: primary, accent, background, panels and text.
- **Support email** and **Currency**
- **Footer text**
- **Terms URL**, **Privacy URL** and **Community URL** (for example your Discord)

Click **Save branding** to apply the changes. They are visible in your store immediately.

> **Tip:** Publish your own terms of service and privacy policy and link them here. Your customers are *your* customers.

## Store behaviour
Under **Store behaviour** you decide what your customers can do:

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Allow customers to register** | Turn it off to stop new sign-ups. |
| **Require email confirmation before ordering** | Customers must confirm their email before they can buy. |
| **Let customers open support tickets** | Enables the support section of your client area. |
| **Server location** | Where new game servers are created: Spain (Europe) or United States. |
| **Max services per customer** | Limit per customer. `0` means unlimited. |

## The customer control panel
Your customers manage their game servers from your client area. Under **Control panel** you choose which parts they get:

| Section | What the customer can do |
|---|---|
| **Power controls** | Start, stop, restart. |
| **Console** | See the live console and send commands. |
| **File manager** | Browse, edit, upload, download, rename, delete, compress and extract files. |
| **Databases** | Create databases, see credentials, change passwords, delete them. |
| **Backups** | Create, restore, download and delete backups. |
| **Startup** | Edit the server's startup variables. |
| **SFTP** | See the SFTP connection details. |
| **Rename** | Rename their own service. |
| **Reinstall** | Reinstall the server from scratch. |

Turning a section off removes the capability completely, not just the button.

> **Warning:** **Reinstall** wipes every file of the service and is the most common cause of "my server is empty" tickets. It is off by default. Turn it on only if your customers are technical.

A service that is **suspended** for an unpaid invoice is read-only: the customer can look at it, but cannot start it or change it until the invoice is paid.

## The VPS control panel
Customers who buy a VPS in your storefront get a dedicated VPS panel:

| Tab | What the customer can do |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | See the IP, username and root password, an SSH command to copy, the browser **console**, live usage, usage graphs of the last 24 hours, monthly traffic and recent activity. |
| **Network** | See the network settings, and add or remove **additional IPv4 addresses**. |
| **Backups** | Create, restore and delete backups, and set up **automatic backups** (daily, weekly or monthly). |
| **Snapshots** | Take quick restore points and go back to them. |
| **Settings** | Change the hostname, use rescue mode, mount ISO images, change firmware and boot order, schedule tasks (start, restart, backup…), **reinstall** and cancel. |
| **Plan & billing** | Renew in advance, **change plan**, see invoices, cancel or undo a cancellation. |

The header always has the power buttons: **Start**, **Restart**, **Shut down** and **Force off**.

Which parts your customers get is decided by the same switches as game servers, plus three VPS-only ones:

| Setting | VPS sections it controls |
|---|---|
| **Power controls** | Power buttons and scheduled tasks. |
| **Live console** | Browser console. |
| **Backups** | Backups and automatic backups. |
| **Reinstall** | Reinstall (off by default). |
| **Rename** | Hostname. |
| **Snapshots** *(VPS)* | Snapshots. |
| **Advanced** *(VPS)* | Rescue mode, ISO images, firmware and boot order. |
| **Plan changes and additional IPs** *(VPS)* | Changing plan and buying extra IPv4 addresses. |

**Price of an additional IPv4**: what your customers pay per extra IP and per renewal period. Leave it empty to charge your cost. It is never charged below your cost.

> **Note:** When a customer changes plan or adds an IP, an invoice is created for the rest of the current period and the change is applied **as soon as it is paid**. From the next renewal on, the new price is part of the renewal invoice.

> **Note:** On your own domain, the console may ask the customer to sign in. The panel then shows a temporary user name and password that only open the console of that VPS and expire after a while.

Remember to click **Save settings**.

## What your customers see
Your client area includes:

- Registration, sign in, password reset and email confirmation.
- The catalogue of plans you have on sale, and checkout with your payment methods.
- Their services, with the control panel sections you enabled.
- Their invoices, and a way to pay them.
- Support tickets, if you enabled them.
- Cancelling a service: customers can only schedule a cancellation for the end of the period they paid for. Deleting a service immediately is reserved to you.

All emails your customers receive (welcome, invoices, payment received, service ready, suspended, etc.) are sent with your store's name and branding.
