# 🚀 Creating your store

## Overview
You manage everything from the **Reseller Panel**, in the sidebar of your GreatHost dashboard. The first time you open it you will see the *Become a reseller* page.

## Step 1: Create the store
Fill in the form and click **Create my store**:

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Store name** | Your brand. Your customers see it everywhere. |
| **Store identifier** | A short name (3-40 characters: lowercase letters, numbers and `-`). It is used in your *preview address*. The form tells you if it is available. |
| **Tagline** *(optional)* | A short sentence shown in your store. |
| **Support email** | Where your customers write to you. It is also used as the sender of your store's emails. |
| **Currency** | EUR, USD or GBP. |

You can change all of this later.

> **Tip:** When the store is created, your price list is pre-filled for you. Review it in **Products & pricing** before going live.

## Step 2: Complete the setup checklist
The **Overview** tab shows a checklist with everything your store needs before it can take real orders:

- Set your store name
- Enable at least one product with a price
- Connect Stripe or PayPal to take payments
- Add a support email address
- Point your own domain at the store *(optional)*

## Step 3: Submit it for review
When the checklist is complete, click **Submit for review**. A GreatHost admin checks new stores before they go live. Once approved, the store status changes to **active** and it starts taking orders.

While your store is waiting for review, only you can open it, through its preview address.

## Your store addresses
| Address | When it works |
|---|---|
| **Preview address** (`https://greathost.es/p/<identifier>`) | Always, even without a domain. Use it to build and test your store. |
| **Live address** | Your own domain once it is connected, otherwise the preview address. |

Both are shown on the **Overview** tab, with a **Copy** button. Use **Open my store** at the top of the panel to see it as your customers do.

## Store status
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **pending** | Being set up or waiting for review. Not taking orders. |
| **active** | Live and taking orders. |
| **suspended** | Paused, by you or by GreatHost. It does not take new orders, but **your customers' existing services keep running**. |
| **rejected** | The application was not approved. Open a support ticket if you need details. |

## Pausing your store
In **Storefront → Pause my store** you can stop taking new orders at any moment, for example during a holiday. Existing services keep working and renewing. Resume it from the **Overview** tab with **Resume my store**.
